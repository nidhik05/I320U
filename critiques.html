Critiques.

Have feedback on a VANTAGE milestone? Here's how to get it to us, and how we respond.

01

How to Submit a Critique

In class

During the milestone presentation itself. Two team members take notes live, one on substance and one on who said what, so nothing gets lost.

After class

Email the team at [team email] with the milestone number and your note. We treat a written critique the same as one given live.

02

What Happens Next

Within 48 hours of a critique, we publish a three-part response on the Milestones & Critique Log section of the home page: the critique itself, what we did about it, and if we did not act on it, why. Critiques we disagreed with still get listed. The person who owned that section of the milestone writes the response, and the write-up role rotates between team members every milestone so the process stays repeatable instead of falling on one person.

Looking for past critiques?

Published critique summaries live with each milestone, not here.
